# Replay Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Replay is a browser extension developed by Jacqueline A. that lets
you select and repeat a range of a YouTube video.

## Information used by the extension

Replay processes the following information locally in your browser:

- The current tab’s URL, to determine whether it is a supported
  YouTube video page.
- The YouTube video identifier, to connect Replay’s popup to the
  correct video and detect navigation.
- Video duration, playback position, playback state, and the start
  and end times you select, to provide looping controls.

Replay does not send this information to the developer or to
external servers.

## Storage and tracking

Replay does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or a user account.

Loop selections are held temporarily in memory. Replay does not
save them to persistent browser storage or synchronize them
between devices.

Replay does not record your browsing history or download,
record, or upload video or audio.

## Permissions

Replay uses the activeTab permission to identify the current
page when you open its browser popup.

Replay runs on www.youtube.com to display its controls and
manage the selected video range.

## Sharing and sale of data

Replay does not sell or share information processed by the
extension with third parties.

Replay’s use of information obtained through Chrome APIs follows
the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited
Use requirements.

## Support emails

If you email replaye.support@gmail.com, your email address and
any information you include will be received through Gmail.
This information is used to respond to your request and resolve
support issues. It is separate from information processed
locally by the extension.

You may request deletion of your support correspondence by
contacting the same address.

## Third-party services

YouTube operates independently of Replay and handles information
under its own privacy policy. This privacy page is hosted by
GitHub Pages, which handles website visits under GitHub’s
privacy statement.

## Changes

This policy will be updated when Replay’s data practices change.
The date above identifies the latest revision.
